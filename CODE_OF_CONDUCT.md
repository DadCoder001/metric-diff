# Code of Conduct

metric-diff follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and focus on the problem rather than the person. This includes how we talk about other projects: when a library behaves differently, describe the difference precisely and kindly.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing others' private information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile, [github.com/DadCoder001](https://github.com/DadCoder001). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban participants, for behaviour they consider inappropriate, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
